SPEECH RECOGNITION

Whisper

Robust Speech → Text at Scale

An encoder–decoder Transformer trained on 680,000 hours of weakly-supervised audio from the web, without fine-tuning on any benchmark.

Key Idea

Scale the data, not the architecture. A plain Transformer trained on huge, noisy, multilingual pairs generalises zero-shot. One decoder handles transcription, translation, language ID and timestamps, and a short token prompt picks the task.
1

Audio → Log-Mel

  • Resample to 16 kHz, pad/trim to 30 s
  • 25 ms window, 10 ms hop
  • 80 Mel bins (128 in large-v3)
  • Log-scale, normalise to ≈[-1, 1]

Output: 80 × 3000 frames

2

Audio Encoder

  • 2 × Conv1D (k=3, GELU), the 2nd with stride 2
  • 3000 → 1500 frames (20 ms each)
  • + sinusoidal positional encoding
  • N pre-LayerNorm Transformer blocks
3

Text Decoder

  • Learned positions, max 448 tokens
  • Causal self-attn → cross-attn to audio
  • Byte-level BPE (GPT-2 style, multilingual)
  • Tied input/output embeddings
4

Multitask Format

The task is just a prefix the decoder reads before writing text:

<|startoftranscript|><|ar|><|transcribe|><|0.00|>text…<|endoftext|>

Swap <|translate|> to get English output. <|nospeech|> enables voice activity detection.

Long-form Decoding

  • Beam search, beam size 5
  • Temperature fallback 0 → 1.0 (step 0.2) if gzip ratio > 2.4 or avg log-prob < -1
  • Silence if P(nospeech) > 0.6
  • Slide the 30 s window using predicted timestamps
  • Condition on the previous window's text
  • Word-level timestamps: align cross-attention with DTW
